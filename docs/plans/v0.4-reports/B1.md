## What changed

- `tools/palette.py` (`just palette`) turns `docs/design/v0.4/palette.json` into `source/ui/palette.c` and `palette.h`: the surfaces per theme, `pill_mix`, and base, tint, ink and on for every accent (the ten items plus the torii glyph's), as RGB15, with an `enum pal_item` in carousel order. It refuses colours not snapped to 15 bits. `tests/host.sh` runs `--check` and fails when the C is stale.
- `struct ui_theme` points at its surfaces and accents and gains `pill_mix`, `backdrop_mix` (carousel backdrop: bg + 6 % / 8 % of base), `float_mix` (popup card: dark + 5 % ink), `bar_shade_mix` (the NOR bar's shades), `struct ui_look` (margins, title bar, content area, 14 px list rows, 12 px Settings rows, pill, card and button radii, edge, shadow offset, scrollbar, hint chips) and `struct ui_motions` (carousel step, open, close, popup, theme wipe: frames and curves, plus the distances spec.md gives). Both built-ins share one `LOOK` and `MOTIONS` block in `ui_theme.c`, so a tweak is one line.
- Palette banks (`ui_theme_banks`, hardware-free): every bank has the eight neutrals at indices 1-8; bank 0 adds the current item (base, tint, ink, on, ground), danger and the floating card at 9-15; bank 1 is bank 0 with BG and PANEL as the item's tint (the selection pill: a row swapped into it); bank 2 the four console inks; bank 3 danger ink and tint and the NOR bar's three shades and ink. `ui_item_set(item)` switches the item, `ui_theme_set(theme)` the theme; neither redraws.
- `motion.h` gains `EASE_OUT_QUAD` and `EASE_SMOOTHSTEP`, which the spec's open card, popup and theme wipe use.
- Deleted (T5, T23; closes E8): `theme_ini.c/.h`, `ui_sdtheme.c`, `sdtheme.h`, the wallpaper and its frame hooks, layout patches (`ui_theme_patch`; only theme.ini made them), THEME's SD option and `/torii/theme.txt`, ACCENT (`ui_accents`, its Settings row, `assress_accent`), `just theme-check`/`theme-new`/`theme-wallpaper`, `tools/theme_check.py`, `tools/themecheck.c`, `asset.py wallpaper`, `tests/themes.sh`, `tests/themecheck_test.py`, `tests/themes/`, `themes/`, `docs/themes.md`, the boot's theme error, `READCACHE_THEME`, the six SD-theme goldens and `settings-theme-held`. `PSRAM_THEME_BASE` is now `PSRAM_RESERVED_BASE`.
- The v0.3 screens keep their `UI_ROLE_*` names, which now name the new slots: they draw in the Paper clean colours (warm paper or graphite, tinted pill with neutral text) until B10 replaces them.

## Deviations

- **The theme wipe's colour run (R7):** the UI's banks are always BG banks 0-3, so every colour a theme switch changes is the one contiguous run BG palette 0-63 (32 words, `UI_THEME_RUN_FIRST`/`UI_THEME_RUN_COLOURS` in `ui.h`); nothing outside it depends on the theme. `palette.py` generates no OBJ banks: the theme-dependent OBJ colours (the carousel's fog, R6) belong in a contiguous run of OBJ banks that B12 lays out, and B9's OBJ pill (R5) takes its tint from the same accent table. With R5's OBJ pill, B9 may no longer need bank 1 as the pill bank; it stays as the v0.3 screens' selection until they go.
- **An item switch loads banks 0 and 1, not only bank 1.** Bank 1 is the selection pill (bank 0's layout with paper as the tint), and bank 0 needs the item's colours too (the v0.3 widgets draw accents in bank 0, and B9's scrollbar thumb, stars and section labels sit on cards). It is 14 colours, in the same VBlank.
- Torii's own accent (`#E74A29`) is generated as an 11th item, `PAL_TORII`, because palette.json has it.
- Layout patches went with theme.ini: nothing else made them. `ui_view_spec.id` stays (32 positional initialisers use it) but is unused; B10 can drop it with the old screens.

## Decisions the user may want to contest

- Values spec.md doesn't give, read off the mockups: shadow offset 1 px, scrollbar 3 px wide, hint chips 10 px high with radius 5, 3 px padding, 2 px from chip to label and 8 px between hints. All in `LOOK` in `source/ui/ui_theme.c`.
- The NOR bar's shades: base, then a third and two thirds of the way to the card (`bar_shade_mix` 85/256), from mockup #16.
- The menu's default item until B10 picks one per page is the SD card's blue (`UI_ITEM_DEFAULT`), like v0.3's default accent.
- The THEME setting stays dark by default; a card set to the old SD theme (value 2) shows dark. ACCENT's slot 29 is retired and never read.
- Until B10: the old screens' file icons take the item's colours (folder in base, cartridges in ink, NES in danger red), and Diagnostics shows passes in plain text (the palette has no green), warnings in the item's ink and failures in danger red.
