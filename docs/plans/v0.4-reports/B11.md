# B11 Popups, confirmations and the letter card

## Branch and commits

`v04-b11-popups`, rebased onto `origin/v0.4` at `7d5e8bd`:

- `753c913` Popups, confirmations and the letter card (B11)
- `e577d54` B11 review fixes: list pixels beside an opening popup, eases clamped, checks

## What changed

- **New `source/ui/ui_popup.c`, with the API in `ui.h`.** It has three overlays on plane 0. Each draws into its own tiles: the whole card while its view is hidden, then 12-line bands drawn in EWRAM and copied in VBlank with `gfx_queue_copy2d`, so nothing tears.
  - **Popup.** A floating card beside a list's selected row. It has a notch at the row, whose pill stays, and is clamped to 21..144−h so it never covers the title bar.
    - It opens in 6 frames: WIN0 grows up and down from the notch's line while BG0 slides in 8 px. It closes the same way in 4 frames.
    - UP/DOWN choose, A picks, B closes. A is ignored until it has opened, so a double press can't pick.
    - API, blocking like `dialog.h`: `ui_popup_open(list, hints, &popup)`, `ui_popup_choose()`, `ui_popup_changed(item)`, `ui_popup_close()`.
    - The popup stays up after A, so Favourite can toggle in place, or a confirmation can open over it.
  - **Confirmation** (`ui_confirm(hints, &confirm)`, which returns 1 for the action).
    - A centred card. Cancel is preselected as a solid pill in the item's base; the action is in danger ink, and solid danger once chosen. LEFT/RIGHT choose, A answers, B cancels.
    - The page dims by BLDY (R9): towards white in light (EVY 8), towards black in dark (EVY 6). Plane 0 is left out of the blend.
    - The card fades in through a palette bank of its own, from the dimmed page colour, over the same 6 frames. On an answer the card leaves at once and the dim fades out in 4.
    - **Over a popup**, the popup stays on screen, dimmed. The confirmation's map holds the popup's entries in a second bank, whose colours follow the hardware's BLDY maths (`ui_colour_bldy`).
  - **Letter card** (`UI_LETTER_NODE(id)` in a list page's layout, then `ui_letter_show(node, letter, prev, next)`).
    - It shows the letter in a solid box, with the letters LEFT and RIGHT would reach beside it.
    - It appears at once, stays `letter_linger` (20) frames after the last jump, and shuts to its middle line in 4 frames with WIN0.
    - The jump maths is in the hardware-free `source/ui/letter.c` (`letter_of`, `letter_jump`, `letter_around`). They are binary searches over a list in `library_sort_key` order, and skip empty letters. "#" covers digits and symbols before A and other scripts after Z, and the jumps wrap.
- **`ui_run.c` (R9).** An overlay (`ui_overlay_set`/`ui_overlay_drop`, `ui_priv.h`) asks for WIN0 while it opens or closes, a BG0 slide, or the confirmation's dim.
  - While WIN0 is the overlay's, the first list clips with WIN1. It also shows inside WIN0 where their lines meet, so it isn't cut beside the notch.
  - The dim is its own branch in the blend chain. A popped plane-0 view drops the overlay.
  - These changes are kept in `overlay_windows()` and a few lines of `regs_update`, apart from B10's areas.
- **Look values.**
  - Every size of the three cards is in `popup_look` at the top of `ui_popup.c`, read off mockups #8–#10.
  - Theme fields: `confirm_dim`, `confirm_brighten`, `motion.letter_linger` and `motion.letter_close`. The popup's open, close and slide fields were already there.
  - Shared sizes come from the theme look: card radius 4, pill 12 px radius 3, buttons radius 7.
- **Smaller changes.**
  - `ui_list_cursor_row` gives where the selected row rests.
  - `ui_hints_get` lets the hint bar's keys be given back.
  - `label_icon` becomes `ui_icon8`.
  - Strings `en_pop_*` are in `lang.c`: choose, ok, close, cancel, Cancel.
  - `NO_COMPAT` gains the new files.
- **UI demo.** A fourth screen, `ui-popup`: an A–Z list of 37 games with letters missing.
  - START opens the game popup. Favourite toggles in place, and Delete asks over the popup; Cancel returns to the popup.
  - A opens a NOR-like 3-item popup whose Format all asks on its own.
  - LEFT/RIGHT jump letters with the card. B switches light and dark.

## Deviations

- **The confirmation is 175×62 at (32, 48), not 172×64 at (34, 46).** With its 1×2 px shadow it fills whole tiles. Over a popup, a part-blank tile would otherwise hide a strip of the popup.
- **The confirmation doesn't open with a window.** It appears while its card fades in (a palette fade) and the page dims, both over `popup_open`'s 6 frames. A window would hide the dimmed popup around it while it grows: one plane holds both.
  - It closes with the card gone at once and the dim fading out in `popup_close`'s 4 frames.
  - R9 rules out an alpha blend for it.
- **The popup and the confirmation are blocking calls**, as dialogs are, not widgets in a page's layout. The letter card is a widget in the page's layout, because the list keeps its keys.
- **Floating cards cast their shadow 1 px right and 2 px down,** as #8 draws it. Page cards keep B9's 1 px down only.
- **Not done (research ideas, not in the ticket):**
  - holding A for Format all (B15 can add it);
  - L/R paging by 7 rows: the user's decision says a screenful (8), and paging stays as B9 has it;
  - the letter-jump key repeat of 20 then 10 frames: B13 sets its keys.

## Decisions the user may want to contest

- **Popup items:**
  - The chosen item is in ink on the tint pill; the others are in ink2, as #9 draws them.
  - Values ("on"/"off") are in the current item's ink. #9 shows Favourites' pink, but only the current item's accent is in the bank.
  - Delete is in danger ink. There's no hairline above it (the mockup has none).
- **Popup layout:** title band 19 px with a hairline at 16, 12 px items, 3 px bottom pad, notch 3 px. Items without a title start 4 px down.
- **Confirmation texts:**
  - The title is in ink and the line under it in ink3, as #10 draws them. ink3 on the card is 3.7:1 in light.
  - Each text is one line, cut with "…".
  - The chosen destructive button is a solid danger fill with card-coloured text.
- **The dim:** in light it brightens towards white, not the paper (R9). White cards stay white and text fades to grey. In dark it darkens 6/16 towards black.
- **Motion:**
  - The popup opens on ease-out quad and closes on ease-out quad from where it is.
  - A confirmation's card fades in over 6 frames and leaves at once on an answer.
  - Of the keys pressed while a popup or confirmation closes (4 frames), only moves (the d-pad, L, R) go to what is under it. A, B, START and SELECT are dropped, so B then A can't play the game under the popup.
  - A is ignored until a popup or confirmation has opened (100 ms).
- **Hint bar while a popup is up:** "^v choose · A ok · B close". While a confirmation is up: "«» choose · A ok · B cancel". It changes one frame after the card appears.
- **Letter card:**
  - Placed as #8: the top right of the list card, 38×38.
  - The neighbours are in ink3, and are the letters LEFT and RIGHT would reach (wrapping).
  - "#" stands for digits and symbols and for other scripts.
  - LEFT goes to the first game of the previous letter, even from inside a letter.
- **In the demo, Cancel on a Delete confirmation returns to the popup.** B13 decides its own flow; I'd suggest the same.

## Bugs

- **Pre-existing, `source/gamename.c:129`:** clang-tidy's `bugprone-not-null-terminated-result` comes and goes between analyze runs. It is a false alarm (the caller NUL-terminates), now in the baseline.
- **Known limits (not bugs), noted in `ui.h`:**
  - While an overlay opens or closes, only one list clips. A page with two resident lists shows the second's spare rows past its rectangle for those 4–6 frames.
  - During those frames, a ring row of the list may peek out for a pixel or two beside the card, past the list's lines.
  - A confirmation's rounded corners over a popup show the dimmed page, not the popup (1–2 px).
  - Plane 0 and plane 2 share character block 0, of which a list page leaves about 16 KB below the list's canvas. A popup (up to 224 tiles), a confirmation (176) and the letter card (36) fit, with little to spare if a page adds much more on planes 0 and 2.
  - A whole popup draws in one frame (143k cycles), a confirmation in 170k. While B3's background scan runs its half-frame job, that frame overruns once, and the card shows a frame later.
  - In light, the hint bar's ink3 labels are brightened with the rest of the page under a confirmation, so they get fainter (the confirmation's keys are still readable as chips).
  - "#" can be two stops per lap: what sorts before A (digits, symbols) and what sorts after Z (other scripts) are separate runs in `library_sort_key` order. Merging them into one stop would need the after-Z run sorted first, or the jumps to treat both as one letter. A decision for B13.
  - Pages must leave plane 0 to the overlays (the popup, confirmation and letter card); `ui.h` says so.
  - The popup and confirmation are blocking calls: close them before launching or leaving the screen. A run whose view was popped behind its back (`ui_leave`, `ui_shutdown`, a launch) is now detected and forgotten (`run_live`: the view must still be on the stack with the run's own spec), and `run_finish` asserts its view is on top.
