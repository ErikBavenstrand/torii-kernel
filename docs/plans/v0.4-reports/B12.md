## What changed

- **`source/screens/home.c`** now shows the carousel instead of B10's list. B10's registry is kept as it was: empty items hide, any number of items, Start on, and B returning to face the item. The title bar has the red torii glyph (`ART_ICON16_TORII`) at (5,1) and the clock at the right, which shows nothing while the clock isn't set. The hints are "«» turn" and "A open". Each turn that comes to rest logs `home: facing WORD`, and tests wait for that line.
- **`source/screens/carousel.c/.h`** is a new widget kind, `UI_CAROUSEL`, registered in `ui_widgets.c`. It takes the whole screen on plane 3; plane 0 is left free for overlays.
  - **Backdrop.** Three BG tiles: sky, a horizon tile at y 74, and floor. Their colours are two new bank 1 roles, `UI_ROLE_SKY` (= GROUND) and `UI_ROLE_FLOOR`. The floor is GROUND moved by the new theme field `floor_step`, in 5-bit steps per channel (`ui_floor_colour`): (−1, −1, 0) in light (the mockup's floor exactly, about "4 % darker") and (+1, +1, +1) in dark, where the mockups' floor is a step lighter than the sky. The roles are built in `ui_theme_banks`, so B19's theme wipe swaps them too. `ui_item_set` now loads banks 0 and 1.
  - **Objects.** Each is an affine 64x64 sprite without double size (R6), with one matrix per object. The front object is first in OAM. The feet are anchored from the pd actually written (`ring_foot_dy`), so they don't bob.
  - **Ground shadows.** Ordinary 32x8 sprite halves, the right half HFLIPped, in B2's six widths, drawn in their object's bank (colour 15).
  - **Fog.** Each object has a fixed OBJ palette bank and affine matrix, chosen by its ring position k modulo 6, kept as long as it shows (so an OAM the commit holds back never shows a neighbour's fog). The bank is mixed toward the current backdrop on every frame the ring moves.
  - **Label.** The 16x16 icon, plus the name (heading font) and count (body font) drawn with the text blitter into columns of 8x16 sprites. It fades by palette and slides 14 px. There are two tile sets, so the hidden one is drawn while the other shows, and the label the ring is turning to is drawn ahead.
  - **Dot strip.** 8x8 sprites, one dot per item in its accent; the front item's dot is a 6x3 pill in its base colour.
  - **Motion and keys.** LEFT/RIGHT step in the theme's 10 frames, ease-out cubic. The backdrop tint crossfades, the label fades out sliding toward the half way point and the next fades in, and the dot moves half way. A held key turns every 8 frames, and each new leg keeps the speed the ring has, so it glides instead of stopping at every item. Quick taps queue up to 2 items ahead. A opens the item the ring is turning to.
  - **Video memory.** Everything is taken when the view is pushed (logged, owned by the view): 8 fixed 2 KB object slots, filled by `gfx_queue_copy` when an object comes within reach of the ring's position or target, so the incoming object is queued on the key press. Nothing is allocated while the ring turns, so a held turn can't fill the resource log. Slots are kept by object. No set or slot is rewritten while an OAM that may use it could still be on screen, including while the commit holds OAM back.
- **`source/screens/carousel_ring.c/.h`** holds the ring's numbers, hardware-free: the window (T10, T26), positions, scale and fog from spec.md's formulas, the affine anchor, and the turning motion. Host tests are in `tests/host/test_carousel.c`.
- **Look values** are in one `carousel_look` table at the top of `carousel.c`, including the ring (`struct ring_look`). The step frames, easing and label slide come from the theme.
- **`carousel_colours(node)`** works the fog, the label's and dots' colours and the backdrop out again in the next update: for B19's theme wipe.
- **Hooks for B19's zoom:**
  - the front object is `obj[0]` of `ring_objects` (front first);
  - its matrix is `car.matrix[ring_item(k, 6)]`, and its bank is the fog bank at the same index;
  - double size needs its own foot anchor, because `ring_foot_dy` assumes a 64 px box: double size puts the centre at 64, not 32.
  - With home up, about 288 OBJ tiles and about 5 OBJ palette banks are left for B19.
- **Tests:** `tests/nav.sh` routes use RIGHT (and LEFT round the end for SD card, NOR, Settings and Info). `tests/nor_safety.sh` checks the menu still answers by turning the carousel.

## Deviations

- **Tiles: fixed slots rather than `art_take_*`/`art_drop`.** B2's unlogged loaders would need a release hook when the view pops (the engine has none, so the blocks would leak). Preloading every object wouldn't scale past 10 items. One logged block of 8 slots, filled by queued copies, meets both aims: nothing is allocated while turning, and the pop frees it all. B2's `art_take_*` stays unused.
- **The label and dots are sprites,** not BG text. That makes the 14 px slide and the fade free (sprite x and a palette), with no engine change to plane scrolling. Text is drawn with the same blitter into column-major 8x16 sprite columns.
- **Held turns aren't strictly ease-out cubic:** each new leg starts with the ring's current speed (a cubic Hermite ending at rest), so a held key glides. A single tap is exactly the theme's 10-frame ease-out cubic.
- **`spec.md` says feet y = 100.** I treat 100 as the ground row under the object, where its shadow is centred. The object stands `lift` = 4 px above it (scaled), as the mockup draws the GBA ending at y 95 over its shadow.
- **`gl_hint_move` ("move") is kept** although home no longer uses it: B13 and B16, running in parallel, show "^v move" from the same base, and deleting it would break their merge.
- **The floor's colour is a step offset per channel, not a mix** (`floor_step` in the theme), as the mockups' floors are one 5-bit step from the sky in each theme.
- **Theme-specific values in the screen's table:** the shadow tone is `shadow_mix[PAL_LIGHT/PAL_DARK]` in `carousel_look` (28 and 156/256 of black into the floor), since only home uses it.
- **No test for the zoom:** the opening zoom is B19's, and A uses the shell's plain fade.

## Decisions the user may want to contest

- **Fewer than five items:** they show nearest first (front, right, left, then the second to the right): four items as offsets −1, 0, +1, +2, and two as front and right. During a step with two to five items, the item leaving at one end fades out while a copy fades in at the other, so it is briefly on screen twice. At rest nothing shows twice. With one item, LEFT/RIGHT do nothing.
- **A reversed tap** (the other way during a step) stops the ring dead and eases it back from where it is.
- **When the items change while home is up** (the library's walk finds a console's first game), the ring snaps to rest on the same item, or the nearest.
- **Neighbours at 0.89 scale**, the far pair at 0.60, as spec.md's formula gives.
- **Held turns:** the first repeat comes after the input's 15-frame delay, then one item every 8 frames (7.5 items a second), gliding. Taps during a step queue up to 2 items ahead. A during a turn opens the item the ring is turning to.
- **The label:**
  - the old one fades out and slides 14 px the way the ring turns until the half way point, then the new one comes in from the other side; the dot jumps at the half way point;
  - while a held turn passes items quickly, an item's label may be missing for one frame at its crossing;
  - name glyphs centred on about y 112, the count at about y 128, the icon 4 px left of the name, and the icon and name centred together.
- **Dots:** each item's base colour faded 25 % toward the backdrop, 2x2 px, 7 px apart. The front item's dot is a 6x3 pill in its full base colour. At most about 18 dots fit the strip (`CAROUSEL_MAX` 14 items).
- **Shadows:** a tone 11 % darker than the floor in light and 61 % darker in dark, sampled off the mockups' shadow colours. Each shadow fogs with its object.
- **Floor:** one 5-bit step from the sky per channel: darker in light, lighter in dark, as in the mockups.
- **Fog:** objects fog toward the sky colour even where they stand on the floor, which is only a step away. In dark, the far objects fog almost to nothing.
- **Counts:** "306 games" on the test card, "1 game", "0 files", "one page" for Settings, nothing for Info (B10's words).
- **Very small far objects:** under 24/256 scale they aren't drawn at all. Only an object entering or leaving is that small, and it is fogged out anyway.

## Bugs

- None open from this ticket.
- **Pre-existing, noted for B19:** `gfx_hold_regs` keeps held bits that weren't dirty at the commit (`gfx.c` clears only `dirty & ~held`), so it can stay set long after any hold ends. Code that reads it as "a hold is on" is wrong. The carousel reads `gfx_dirty_regs & GFX_DIRTY_OAM` instead. Nothing reads it that way today.
