## What changed

- **New `source/screens/nor.c`/`.h`**, registered in `nav.c` (mockups #16, #17).
  - One card holds two things:
    - the storage line, "40 MB used" and "24 MB free of 64", over B9's storage bar, one NOR-amber shade per game;
    - the games in the order they were written, cleaned names (B4) and sizes, 14 px rows, four at a time with a scroll bar past four.
  - Format all is a card of its own under it: the chip icon, "Format all" in danger ink, and "erases every game".
  - The title bar shows the position (`2/4`; Format all counts as a row).
- **Keys:**
  - UP/DOWN go through the games, then on to Format all. A fresh press wraps at the ends, as every list does. The list doesn't take keys: the page moves its cursor and hides the list's pill while Format all is chosen. Format all's card then lifts 1 px in the item's tint.
  - On a game:
    - A launches it. L held with A gives a hard reset, as in v0.3.
    - START opens the popup: Launch, Game info and Delete (danger, last).
  - On Format all, A asks first: "Format all of NOR?" / "3 games go. About 4 minutes." (the 4-minute warning is back), with Cancel preselected.
  - B goes back to home.
  - Hints: "A launch · START menu · B back" on a game, "A format · B back" on Format all.
- **Delete** takes only the latest game, because the NOR keeps its games back to back.
  - Picked on another game, it shows "Only the last game on NOR can be deleted." in a dialog (the v0.3 message, re-added).
  - On the latest game it asks over the popup: "Delete Minish Cap?" / "Its save on the SD card stays.", Cancel preselected. Cancel goes back to the popup.
  - Before deleting, it reads the NOR's list again and deletes only if the latest game is still the one chosen. Otherwise it says "The NOR changed: nothing was deleted."
  - A "Deleting from NOR" notice shows during the erase. The page then comes back on the game before.
- **Game info** is a dialog: the cleaned name as its title, then the file name, the size and save type ("16 MB · SRAM"), and "Save states on" when set.
- **Engine and shell changes:**
  - `ui_list_mark(node, shown)` (`ui_list.c`, `ui.h`) hides a sprite-pill list's pill and stops its marquee.
  - `nav_reshow()` (`nav.c`, `nav.h`) fades the place out and back in after a deferred action that changed it. The test hook now re-shows an open page after its action.
  - The storage bar draws a part under a pixel as one pixel.
  - `action_launch` reads the NOR's list again after a write to NOR. Before, `game_total_NOR` stayed stale and home's NOR count was wrong.
  - An older kernel's list entries are NUL-terminated as they load.
- **Layout:**
  - It is built into EWRAM when the page opens. The games card is as tall as its rows (one to four) and Format all follows it.
  - Every value is in `nor_look`, or in two `#define`s for the list config, which needs constant expressions.
  - Planes:
    - plane 1: the list alone;
    - plane 2: Format all's texts and the scroll bar, both transparent. Plane 2 shares tiles with the popup and confirmation, which ran out of room when the title was there.
    - plane 3: everything else.
  - Format all's two cards (plain and lifted) stay shown, and the one dirtied last is the one that shows.

## Deviations

- **One card for storage and games, not two.** The title bar's rectangle (0–24, whole tiles) can't share a plane with a card that starts above y 24. Plane 2 can't hold the title either: its tiles are shared with the popup and the confirmation, and the confirmation over the popup ran out of BG VRAM. So the card starts at y 24 (#16 has it at about 18), on plane 3 with the title, and holds both the storage line and the games. The text-over-bar look, the games and the separate Format all card are as #16.
- **No coloured dots before the games.** 14 px rows can't draw icons (`ui_list` draws them only at 16 px or more). The bar's shades still follow the order of the rows.
- **Four rows visible**, not as many as fit. A list's viewport must be whole tiles and whole rows, so it is 56 or 112 px, and 112 doesn't fit with the storage line and Format all.
- **Hold A for Format all: not done** (left to B15 by B11; research-v04-ux.md). The confirmation with Cancel preselected, plus a 4-minute erase, is guard enough, and it keeps every destructive action on one pattern.
- **L/R don't page** on the NOR page: L is the hard-reset modifier (L+A), and four rows hardly need paging.
- **Game info is a dialog**, not a reading card: B13's reading card wasn't there to reuse.

## Decisions the user may want to contest

- **Layout:**
  - The storage and games card starts at y 24, with the storage text 2 px under the card's top.
  - Format all's card has its own 18 px body.
  - Chosen, Format all's card lifts 1 px all round in the item's tint (NOR amber). #17 shows a pinkish tint: the danger tint isn't in bank 0.
- **Numbers:**
  - Games are listed in written order, not A–Z, so the bar's segments and Delete's "latest" line up with the rows.
  - The storage line rounds used up and free down, so they add up to 64 ("61 MB used" for 60.1 MB). Under 1 MB it shows KB.
  - Sizes are rounded to the nearest MB: a game's area includes its completion-mark block.
- **Names:** the cleaned file name, not names.txt custom names. NOR entries have no SD path to key them on.
- **Delete:**
  - It is shown on every game; picked on one that isn't the latest, it explains instead of acting.
  - Cancel on its question returns to the popup.
  - After a delete, the cursor lands on the game before.
- **Format all:**
  - It is offered on an empty NOR too ("It takes about 4 minutes."): it also clears leftovers an older kernel left.
  - The question's text is "3 games go. About 4 minutes." ("Saves on SD stay." didn't fit with the time on one line).
  - The page opens on Format all when there are no games.
- **Texts:** "No games on NOR", "Deleting from NOR", "Its save on the SD card stays.", "The NOR changed: nothing was deleted.", and the hints "launch", "menu", "format".
- **Position:** the title's position counts Format all as a row ("4/4" on it).

## Bugs

- **A pressed on a game while the list is mid page change is dropped** (`nor.c`, `nor_event`). It happens right after a wrap, for about 8 frames. The list's own A queues it. Repro: on the NOR page with 6 games at the last row, DOWN DOWN (to Format all, then the wrap to the first game), then A within 8 frames: nothing launches.
- **A half-erased game dropped after a power cut logs a "kernel error: ... dropped" line on every boot until the next list save.** This is pre-existing (`GetFileListFromNor`), and the adversarial tester saw it after power cuts during a page delete. Games and positions stay correct.
- **Behind Delete's question, the popup's edge and its Delete row show beside the confirmation.** This is B11's design (the popup stays, dimmed), noted only because it looks busy on the NOR popup.
- **`tests/nav.sh`'s page routes assume the menu starts on home.** On a cart left on a page for 5 s (Last place), set Start on to Home first (slot 32 = 2), as `nor_safety.sh`, `loader_safety.sh` and `nor.sh` now do.
