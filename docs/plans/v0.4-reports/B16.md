## What changed

- **The page** (`source/screens/settings_page.c`, registered in `nav.c`). It draws B6's table as one scrolling page of section cards. The selected row's explanation sits in the hint bar, centred. Values are plain words, and the title shows "5/25".
  - **Editing:**
    - A edits a value in place as « value »: LEFT/RIGHT step it (only Dim screen repeats when held), A keeps it, B puts back the value from before A.
    - Time and Date open the field card (#20).
    - Hotkeys show as key chips and are set by pressing the combination: the largest set held for 18 frames wins, offered once every key is up. Nothing for 5 s, or B alone, cancels. A refusal shows its reason in danger ink for 90 frames.
    - LED colours show as swatches; while edited, all eight are shown and the hint names the colour.
  - **Live previews:** the theme (`ui_theme_set`, an instant switch until B19) and the cart's LED (`Set_LED_control`, as CheckSwitch does).
  - **Layout follows the values:** LED on/off adds or removes the colour rows (laid out again, cursor kept, scroll kept). Save states, Cheats, Reset and Sleep rename the key rows (redrawn).
  - **Actions:** Rescan library (B3's `library_rescan`; "Scanning..." until it ends) and Identify library (B21's `names_identify`; its progress "12/176" as the value).
  - **L/R** jump to the previous or next section.
- **Writing.** Nothing is staged while the page is up: previews set the theme and the LED directly. The working copy (EWRAM) is staged and committed in one go when the page is left, through a new `nav_page.leave` hook.
  - `nav.c` calls the hook in `show()` after the screen has gone out (B, any place change, the re-show after an action) and before a test-hook action (a launch).
  - So every exit commits, and no staged copy is ever left behind. An edit still open at an exit is put back first, with its preview.
  - The NOR is written only if a slot changed (`Commit_SET_info`, `Save_info`).
  - Turning LOGGING on starts the background open (`tlog_flush_soon`), as v0.3 did.
  - The log is flushed as the page opens (the v0.3.2 hang lesson).
- **Scrolling cards (the open question).** Each section is three kinds of 12 px slot in one list: its label, its rows, and a gap under its card.
  - The list's model paints every slot whole (new `ui_list_model.paint`): the card's part of it (rounded top in the label slot, body behind rows, rounded bottom and shadow in the gap), the pill, the label and the value.
  - So cards, their edges and section labels scroll with the rows by the pixel on one plane, clipped by the list's window. No BG2 canvas or pinned cards were needed.
  - The pill is drawn into the selected row (`UI_LIST_DRAWN_PILL`), since a sprite pill would sit behind the cards.
  - The swatches are sprites (`ui_list_model.objs`) in an OBJ palette bank of their own: BG banks have no room for seven LED colours.
- **UI additions:**
  - `ui_list_recount` (rows change in place, scroll kept).
  - A field card in `ui_popup.c` (`ui_fields_open` / `ui_fields_key` / `ui_fields_changed`; it opens and closes like a popup, with 4 hint keys).
  - `ui_hints_note`: the hint text's ink and a countdown bar. Hint text is now centred.
  - `ui_round_inset`, `ui_icon8` and `ui_obj_add` move to `ui.h` for models that paint.
- **Hardware-free layout and capture** (`source/screens/settings_layout.c`), host-tested.
- **Model:** Reset hotkey's explanation reads "On anyway with Save states or cheats." while either is on. `settings_value_text` copies plain values instead of calling snprintf.
- **Strings:** `en_set_scanning`, `en_set_press`, `en_set_press_hint`, `en_set_refused[]` and `en_hint_change/done/field/set`.

## Deviations

- **The view starts at y 24, not the mockup's 20:** rectangles are whole tiles. The first section's capitals are 2 px lower than drawn.
- **12 px slots between sections:** a section is label + rows + gap slot, so sections sit 5 px further apart than the mockup's 2 px of paper. This is needed for a uniform 12 px list.
- **The pill jumps** to the selected row; it doesn't slide (it is drawn into the row, see above).
- **Swatch edges:** coloured swatches are sprites with a darker edge, as specified; White's and Off's edges are drawn in ink3.
- **Hint chips are right-aligned** as everywhere else (B9), not centred as in the mockup. The explanation line is centred.
- **No live preview of Dim screen:** it applies once written.
- **Settings is the only page with `nav_page.leave`;** the other pages pass 0.
- **Identify library** shows "done/total" as its value while it runs. The mockup predates B21's row.

## Decisions the user may want to contest

- **L/R jump to the previous or next section.** L first goes back to the current section's first row. Both wrap.
- **Steps on held keys:** LEFT/RIGHT step only on a press, except Dim screen, which repeats when held. The Theme preview never flickers on a held key.
- **The theme switches the instant RIGHT is pressed** (B goes back); the wipe is B19's.
- **The cart's LED follows the LED rows live** while they are edited (the LED colour, the SD light colour, on/off), and B puts it back.
- **Hotkey capture:** the 18-frame hold; 5 s with nothing held cancels (keys held keep it waiting); a too-brief tap waits on; up to four held keys are shown as chips; B held alone cancels.
- **Refusal texts:**
  - "Already used by Reset keys." (or by the other row's current label, e.g. "Load state keys")
  - "Use 2 or 3 keys."
  - "Use L, R, START or SELECT too."
  - "START+SELECT wakes a sleeping game."
  - "Games reset on A+B+START+SELECT."
- **Reset hotkey explanation** while Save states or Cheats is on: "On anyway with Save states or cheats."
- **Opening Settings from home starts at Theme;** Last place at boot restores the row. A hidden or unknown row gives Theme.
- **Leaving writes after the screen has gone out:** the NOR write happens on a black screen between the fades, so leaving after a change shows a brief black pause while the sector is erased and written.
- **Time and Date write the cart's clock at once** when A sets them in the field card. They aren't staged with the other values, and B on the page doesn't undo them.
- **Edits are lost if the power goes off before leaving the page:** they are written only as it is left.
- **A failed NOR write gives no message:** the live theme (and LED) stay as previewed for the session, and the next boot reads what the NOR holds.
- **B14's "showing the first N" note is centred**, like every hint-bar text line.
- **Rescan library** shows "Scanning..." until the library is idle again (that includes its index write).

## Bugs

- **Pre-existing, found by the adversarial tester:** a power cut in the middle of a settings write (`kernel:program:1`) leaves the whole settings sector erased, and every setting reads its default after. That is how `Save_info` has always worked: one sector, erased then programmed, no second copy.
- **A failed NOR write isn't retried within the visit:** the page treats the values as saved. Re-entering reloads from the NOR.
- **`ui_hints_get` doesn't give back a hint note's look,** so a popup over a danger or countdown line restores it as plain text. It can't happen on Settings today.
- **The held-scroll golden hashes every frame,** so code-layout changes that move draw timing re-record it.
