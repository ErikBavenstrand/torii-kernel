## What changed

- **Opening an item (E7, R8).** A on home zooms the front object into its page (`UI_TR_OPEN`, `ui_zoom_set`):
  - **The sprite.** The carousel hands over the front object as it shows (`carousel_zoom`): sprite, box centre, scale, colours and sky. The UI draws it with a sprite, palette bank and affine matrix of its own, taken once per session in `ui_init` (64 OBJ tiles, 1 bank, 1 matrix).
    - So home and the page never need to be resident together, and the page gets the whole of VRAM (B12's 288-tile limit doesn't apply).
    - Home goes after two frames: the first puts the sprite in place, and the second's VBlank shows it alone. The page is then pushed and drawn hidden.
  - **The zoom (theme `open_zoom`, 5 frames, ease-out quad).** The sprite is double size from its first frame, so that frame is pixel-identical to the carousel's. It scales from 1.0 to `open_scale` 1.9 about the middle row of its drawn part, and fogs into the backdrop. Meanwhile the backdrop (palette 0) goes from the item's tint to the paper.
  - **The title bar (theme `open`, 12 frames, ease-out cubic; `open_slide` 128 px).** It shows as soon as the page is drawn and its uploads are done. Its heading (and icon) slide in from the left, redrawn shifted each frame.
    - The title bar's plane also holds the hint bar, so it can't scroll part of itself. R8's BG0 would need the title on a plane popups own, and the research's other options were a VCOUNT split or 8 px map steps.
    - The right-hand position or clock stays put, as `motion.png` draws it.
    - The heading's paint window now starts at column 0 (18 more BG tiles per title).
  - **The card (theme `open_card`, 7 frames, ease-out quad; `open_rise` 12 px).** Once the sprite has gone and the page is ready, the page's planes alpha-blend in over the paper, the text over its card, while the card plane rises.
    - The title band is WIN0 with no effects.
    - The list clips with WIN1 meanwhile.
    - The title bar's plane is moved in front of the card for the rise, so the card never blends with the hints under it.
    - The page takes keys from then on.
- **B (theme `close_card` 5 frames ease-in cubic, then `close_zoom` 5, ease-out quad): the open backwards.**
  - The card falls and fades and the heading slides out. Then the sprite comes back from 1.9 out of the fog while home draws hidden (`carousel_zoom_rest`).
  - Home shows, sprites and all, in the frame the sprite goes, once its uploads are done.
  - If the item went while its page was open, B is a plain fade to home facing the nearest item.
  - B during the open goes back from where the open is.
  - Any other screen change during a zoom carries on from what shows.
- **The theme wipe (R7, T18).**
  - `ui_theme_switch(theme)` loads the new theme and puts the old one's colours (BG palette 0–47 and the UI's sprite bank) into a **raster split** (`gfx_set_split`, new in `engine/gfx`).
  - Each VBlank the commit writes the new colours, then arms a VCOUNT interrupt on the line before the edge. Its handler, `gfx_split_irq` (36 bytes of ARM in IWRAM), starts DMA0 and DMA1 in HBlank mode. They copy the old colours (32 words, from 128 bytes of IWRAM, `gfx_split_data`) in that line's HBlank, so the edge and everything below it stays old.
  - The edge comes down over theme `theme_wipe` (16 frames, smoothstep). It is a hard edge: a palette swap changes whole lines.
  - Asked again for the theme it is leaving, the edge goes back up.
  - **Settings' Theme row now previews with the wipe** (B16 merged, wired in `settings_page.c`). The UI demo's B on ui-popup does too.
  - Jobs don't run during the wipe, as a card read's DMA3 would hold the interrupt back.
- **E9.** A dialog shown with no view up comes in from black, rising 8 px (new transition kind `UI_TR_CARD`, 10 frames). This covers the loading card, the boot's notice, and a launch's questions and errors.
- **E10.** `ui_leave` no longer pops a dialog at once. It goes to black with its screen: the dim under it deepens until the fade passes it, then everything darkens together.
- **E11.** The same rule when coming in: a menu or dialog that comes up with its screen darkens with it, rather than showing at full over it.
- **E6.** The mosaic transitions are removed, with the engine's unused mosaic register.
- **E5 (cross-fade with both screens resident)** is answered by the zoom, which carries home's object across the change. A true cross-fade of two full screens isn't possible: both use all four planes and most of BG VRAM.
- **Engine fix.** A hold left over from an earlier page (`gfx_hold_regs`, B12's note) stalled registers and OAM behind any later upload. The hold now ends once the queue is empty. This was causing a repeated frame in the zoom.
- **Tear watch (`emu/mgba-shot.c`).** A store made by an HBlank-timed DMA is not a tear: it lands between two lines. CPU stores in HBlank still count.
- `nav.c`: for the open, the settings and counts are read before the screen goes, so the page push doesn't overrun a frame and stall the sprite. Otherwise they are read after the page's leave (Settings' write), as before.
- `docs/release-checklist.md`: two cart checks, the open and close, and the wipe's edge.

## Deviations

- **R8:** the title bar slides by being redrawn shifted on its own plane, not on BG0. Popups own BG0, and the hint bar shares the title's plane, so a scroll or split would move or cut it. It costs a heading redraw a frame, about 10k cycles, only during a zoom.
- **R7:** a hard edge, no interleaved lines. Dithering needs a second colour set in IWRAM (another 128 bytes), and the IWRAM margin is about 2 KB.
- The wipe also swaps the UI's sprite bank at the edge. Other sprite banks switch at once: Settings' LED swatches (their Off outline follows ink3), and a popup's own banks if one were open.
- **The open's title slide** starts when the page is drawn (2–4 frames in) and runs over what the open has left, at least half of it, so the open ends around frame 12–14 rather than exactly 12. A page that takes long to draw (Info, about 219k cycles) repeats the zoom's first frame once.
- **The close is 5 + 5 frames, not a 10-frame mirror.** The page has to be popped before home is pushed (the two never coexist), so the title bar can't still be sliding while the object zooms back.
- **The close from Settings** waits for Settings' NOR write on the bare paper, between the card going and the object coming back, as the fade waited on black.
- `nav.c`'s show() reads the settings and counts before `ui_leave` for the open only.

## Decisions the user may want to contest

- **The open:**
  - the object zooms about the middle of its drawn part, in place (not toward the screen's centre);
  - the floor under the carousel's horizon (one 5-bit step from the sky) vanishes when the zoom starts, as the backdrop becomes palette 0;
  - the other carousel objects, label, dots, torii and hints vanish at once when the zoom starts (and appear at once at the close's end);
  - the page's hint bar fades in with the card;
  - the title heading comes from 128 px left;
  - the right-hand position or clock is static;
  - list sprites (pill, marks) appear half way through the card's fade, as sprites can't take the planes' blend.
- **A light-theme artefact during the card's fade:** text blends toward its card's white before the card itself is solid, so for the first frame or two it looks a little lighter than the paper. This is the research's accepted approximation.
- **The close's timing:** the card falls and fades and the title slides out with ease-in cubic over 5 frames, then the object comes back over 5 frames with ease-out quad.
- **The theme wipe:** a hard edge, smoothstep, 16 frames. Flipping Theme again mid-wipe sends the edge back up.
- **Dialogs in a session of their own** (the loading card, the boot notice, a launch's errors) fade in from black, rising 8 px over 10 frames. Settling the card adds about 10 frames to a launch's start.
- **A dialog left up when its screen changes** now fades to black with the screen instead of vanishing first.

## Bugs

- **The split needs a commit every VBlank.** A frame that overruns while the wipe runs shows the old theme over the whole screen for that frame. That is documented in `gfx.h`; jobs are held off during the wipe and wipe frames are about 30k cycles, but Settings' first wipe frame is 118k. It is a cart check (`docs/release-checklist.md`).
- **The cart may show a ragged edge** if the VCOUNT interrupt runs late (a long DMA3 such as an SD read). mGBA can't show this; it is on the release checklist.
- **Pre-existing:** loading-card launches go black for about 8 frames between home's fade-out and the card's rise (the launch's own work). The fade-out itself is 2 visible frames.
- **A pressed during the close** is held and opens the page again once home shows. This is intended (keys pressed while a screen goes are the next one's).
