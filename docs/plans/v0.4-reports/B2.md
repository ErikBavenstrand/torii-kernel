# B2 Icons, sprites and placeholder art

**Branch and commits:** `claude/v04-b2-assets-77079f`, on `origin/v0.4` (`7b3ec7f`, with B1 and B6). The work was squashed into one code commit while rebasing onto B1, to resolve its conflicts once:

- `3fee70f` Menu art as engine assets: icons, carousel objects, shadows, placeholder box (B2), including both review rounds
- the commit adding this report

**What changed:**

- The icons and carousel sprites moved from `docs/design/v0.4` to `assets/ui/` (`icons16/`, `icons8/`, `sprites/`). New sources: `shadows/w16.png`–`w56.png` and `box.png`. `assets/ui/art.toml` lists the carousel items (icon, marker, object) and pairs the 16x16 icons into palette banks.
- `just assets` runs `tools/asset.py ui`. It writes `source/ui/art_data.c` and `art_ids.h` (generated, committed, in `.clang-format-ignore`). `tests/asset_test.py` fails when they are stale. Redrawing art is a PNG edit plus `just assets`; adding an icon also needs one line in `icon16_banks`.
- `source/ui/art.h` and `art.c` are the API. Screens get art by id (`ART_ICON16_*`, `ART_ICON8_*`, `ART_SPRITE_*`, `ART_ITEM_*`, `art_shadows`) and never name tile offsets:
  - `art_load_icon16`, `art_load_sprite`, `art_load_shadow` and `art_load_box` allocate engine blocks shared by tag, owned by the current view, and queue the upload.
  - `art_take_sprite`, `art_take_shadow` and `art_drop` are for the carousel's ring, which loads and drops objects as it turns. They take blocks outside the allocation log, as the cover streamer does, so a long run of turns never fills `RES_LOG_CAP`.
  - `art_sprite_colours(sprite, ground, out)` gives an object's 16 colours with the ground tone in colour 15. The sprite palettes leave colour 15 at 0, so the screen must write the tone there.
  - `art_icon8_tile` expands a 1-bit 8x8 icon into a 4bpp BG tile in any colour index.
  - `art_shadow_for(width)` picks a shadow width.
- Formats:
  - 16x16 icons: 4bpp OBJ tiles, two to a bank.
  - 8x8 icons: one colour; the PNG's shade is only a preview.
  - Carousel objects: 64x64 4bpp, 1:1, centred, standing on row 63, at most 14 colours. Index 15 is kept for the shadow tone, so a shadow drawn in its object's bank fogs with it.
  - Shadows: one 32x8 half per width, drawn twice with HFLIP, as ordinary sprites (R6).
  - Placeholder box: 80x80 BG tiles in three recolourable slots (band, body, shape), sized to B22's 80x80 art. `art_load_box(charblock, &bank)` loads the tiles into a BG character block and the colours into a BG bank of the box's own, and returns the first tile number; B13 maps the 100 tiles in order, 10 a row, and recolours the band.
  - **The box is BG, while `cover.c` streams covers into OBJ slots.** B22 must reconcile them: draw real art as BG too, or give the box an OBJ layout.
- Credits: KyleBing (CC BY 4.0) is credited in `art_data.c`, `assets/ui/README.md` and a new Credits section in `README.md`, which also credits Galmuri. The `docs/design/v0.4/README.md` credit now points to the new paths. `CLAUDE.md` gained one line on `just assets`.
- The test kernel has an art sheet: power on with SELECT+R held, then press A for each next page.

**Deviations:**

- **Sprites aren't resampled to 52 px.** Resampling pixel art by uneven factors breaks outlines, so the objects are drawn at the size they show at the front (scale 1.0). Drawn sizes:
  - GBA 64x40, NES 62x30
  - GB and GBC 38x59
  - Recent 52x40, NOR 46x34, Favourites 44x30
  - Settings 40x40, Info 34x42, SD 30x40

  The average longest side is about 51 px.
- Only the GBA (82x48) and the NES (80x38) were too big for a 64x64 sprite. I narrowed them by removing duplicate pixel columns and rows, keeping the buttons and D-pad intact. The GBA lost its tiny green LED.
- **Generated data is in `art_data.c` and `art_ids.h`, not `art.c`.** `art.c` holds the hand-written helpers and `art.h` the hand-written API, so the API docs don't live in the generator.
- **The placeholder box is 80x80 BG tiles, not an OBJ.** It is sized to B22's art format, and 80x80 is no sprite size.
- **`docs/design/v0.4/sprites/cont.png` is deleted.** It was a "continue" variant that no item uses; it is in git history at `788f906`.
- **The Game Boy object has 14 colours instead of 15.** I merged one 1-pixel colour (#383840 → #202020) to free index 15 for the shadow.

**Decisions the user may want to contest:**

- The GBA and NES objects are narrowed redraws (GBA 64x40, NES 62x30), and the GBA is the largest object at the front. Sizes vary by object instead of all being normalised to 52 px: the SD card is 30x40 and the Game Boys 59 px tall.
- Ground shadows come in six widths (16–56 px), 3–6 px tall: a solid core with a checker-dithered rim, all in one colour. The shadow is drawn in its object's bank, so it fogs with the object.
- The placeholder box is 80x80: a band across the top (the console's base colour), then a body with a darker hill and ground. Colours are recoloured per screen. B13/B22 put the text (console name, initials) over it.
- The 8x8 icons take their colour from the screen; their PNG colour is ignored. The 8x8 GBA and Info icons are used as they are, per the ticket.
- The 16x16 icons keep their PNG colours: they follow neither the item's base colour nor the theme, in light and dark alike.
- The placeholder box's default colours are GBA indigo #4A39B5 (band), a muted red #D6736B (body) and a darker red #9C524A (hill and ground). Screens recolour them per console.
- The torii glyph is the 16x16 icon `ART_ICON16_TORII` (vermilion with a black kasagi). It has an OBJ palette bank of its own, which nothing shares.
