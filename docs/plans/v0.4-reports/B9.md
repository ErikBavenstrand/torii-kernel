## What changed

- **Lists at any pitch from 8 to 32 px (research override R5), measured first.** A list's row slots lie back to back in one column-major canvas, `period = k * rh` lines tall. k is rounded up so the period is whole tile rows: 14 px rows in 112 px give k 12 and 168 lines. The list's plane gets a 32x64 map, written once, in which map row m shows canvas tile row m % (period / 8). `BG VOFS = scroll % period - top` (`ring_vofs`) alone scrolls it, and no map entry changes while it scrolls.
  - The text is drawn by the existing pixel-y blitter. Rows share tiles but never a word.
  - `ring.h` has the generalised maths. `ui_list.c` was rewritten onto it: B7's cache calls now pass the canvas stride (`ct * 8`) and the slot's line offset, and `ROW_COPY` was re-timed.
  - The page change, glide, marquee, row cache and predraw keep B7's behaviour.
- **The selection:**
  - **A list without paper** (spec paper 0) draws transparent rows over a card on a plane behind. Its selection is a pill of 32x16 sprites (left cap, middles, right cap; 24 OBJ tiles shared by every list), set between the list's text and its card by OBJ priority.
  - **The pill slides** to the cursor's row on a spring of its own (`motion.pill_spring`, `SPRING_FAST`; 0 jumps) and is clamped into the viewport.
  - **During page fades** it goes semi-transparent with the plane. It hides while the page is hidden.
  - **A list with paper** (every v0.3 screen, menus on a panel) draws the pill under the selected row's text instead, redrawn when the cursor moves.
  - **Palette:** bank 1 (the v0.3 palette-swap selection) is gone, so the UI's banks are 0-2 and the theme run is 48 colours.
- **Rows redrawn while on screen** (`ui_list_refresh`, the drawn pill, marquee steps) are drawn into an EWRAM pool (5 KB). They are then copied over their slot in VBlank by `gfx_queue_copy2d`, one queue entry for a row's runs.
  - The 2D entry is run from ROM by `gfx_commit_vram`. The IWRAM loop `gfx_commit_queue` only gained a stop at it (a few bytes; no other IWRAM code was added).
  - `text_clear` fills columns of 8 or more words by DMA, and the row cache copies columns by DMA. The cache's word loop from ROM made a cached 14 px row slower to copy (8.7k) than to clear (5.1k).
- **Sprites in the UI.** `ui_frame` rebuilds OAM around the widgets' update (`ui_obj_add`), so a widget that stops adding its sprites hides them.
  - **Priority levels** (`obj_levels`): each view's planes get a level from the top down. A view with sprites takes two: its front planes (its list's plane and plane 0), then its sprites and its other planes.
  - **Sprite palette:** the UI's sprite bank (`ui_obj_bank`) is a copy of BG bank 0 and follows theme and item switches.
  - **Blending:** dims, dialog fades, DIM and transitions take the sprites along. Where a blend can't take them (no second target under a sprite on its own card), they hide: while their dialog fades in or out, and past the middle of a screen's alpha fade.
  - **Timing:** OAM waits with DISPCNT and the scrolls while those wait for the queue. `DISP_OBJ` goes off while a transition's screen is gone, and `ui_shutdown` clears the UI's sprites.
- **New widgets** (`source/ui/ui_paper.c`), every size in `LOOK` in `ui_theme.c`:
  - `UI_CARD`: a rounded card body inset from its tile rectangle, so it can sit off the grid. It has a 1 px shadow below (no right shadow, as in the mockups) and an optional hairline (`UI_CARD_EDGE`, for floating cards). The card uses 9 tiles, shared by tag.
  - `UI_TITLE`: the 16x16 icon sprite from B2's art, the heading, and a right-hand text that `ui_list_page_label` can change with the page. Both texts have two tile sets.
  - `UI_HINTS`: key chips with labels from the right edge in, or one explanation line (T25).
  - `UI_PATH`: path pills. Parents sit in a paper pill, the current folder in a tinted pill, and leading folders give way to "...".
  - `UI_STORAGE`: parts in the NOR bar's three shades on a soft track.
  - Labels take an 8x8 icon (section labels, `icon8`).
  - The scroll bar has a 1 px track in the hairline colour and a 3 px thumb in the item's base, at the look's `scrollbar_x`.
  - The reading view (`source/ui/ui_reading.c`, hardware-free) wraps a long text into rows with headings and key chips, for a list with `UI_LIST_PLAIN | UI_LIST_SCROLL`. With `UI_LIST_SCROLL`, UP and DOWN scroll a row.
- **`ui_list_config`** gains `pad` (the text inset) and the row pitches `UI_ROW_PITCH_LIST` and `UI_ROW_PITCH_SETTINGS`, taken from the look. `ui_item` gains `key`/`key_len` (reading rows).
- **Caps:**
  - `RES_BG_BLOCKS` 24 → 40 (a Paper page has a dozen widgets, two tile sets each).
  - `RES_LOG_CAP` 48 → 64.
  - `UI_ARENA_BYTES` 1024 → 1536 (a list's state is about 256 bytes).
  - Plane 3's tiles come from the top of their block, so they don't split the run a plane-1 list needs.
- **Contrast (B1's findings), `palette.json` regenerated with `just palette`:**
  - Light ink3 `#9C948C` → `#8C847B`: hint labels on the paper went from 2.63 to 3.24:1, ink3 on a card from 2.99 to 3.68:1.
  - Dark NES/danger ink `#E76B73` → `#EF7B84`: danger on the dark floating card went from 4.03 to 4.68:1.
  - New tests in `tests/host/test_theme.c`:
    - ink3 on the paper and on a card at 3:1 or more;
    - ink2 on the soft chip at 3:1 or more;
    - danger on the floating card at 4.5:1 or more, in both themes;
    - each item's ink on its own tint at 3:1 or more (marks only).
  - Text on a pill (the current path pill too) is the neutral ink, as spec.md says.

## Deviations

- **R5's design held without the fallback:** 14 px rows scroll at 60 fps with the canvas (the worst held-scroll frame is 29 % of a frame), so no HBlank DMA was needed.
- **The pill sprites draw for transparent lists only.** Lists with paper (the v0.3 screens and their menus) draw the pill under their text. Sprites can't sit under opaque text, and those screens go in B10. Popups (B11) can use either.
- **`row_h` 0 still means the theme's `row_h` (16).** The new pages ask for the look's pitch with `UI_ROW_PITCH_LIST` / `_SETTINGS`, so the v0.3 screens keep their 16 px rows until B10.
- **The row cache stays at 24 KB.** At 14 px a cached row (6.6k) costs about a third of a drawn one (about 22k), and the cache is what lets a page change draw ahead (B7, E3). Shrinking it to one page (about 15 KB) would free 9 KB of EWRAM if needed.
- **Card shadows fall down only (1 px), with no hairline on page cards,** as the mockups draw them. spec.md says "card hairlines"; `UI_CARD_EDGE` adds one, for floating cards.
- **Hint chips use radius 3** (spec/B1: 5), which reproduces the mockup's corners (insets 2, 1, 0). The one rounding rule (`ui_round_inset`, r² + r − 1) gives 2,1,0 at r3 and 3,1,1,0 at r4, as the mockups draw them.
- **The demo's v0.3 progress bar and status panel are gone from the UI demo.** `tests/kernel/test_ui.c` still covers the progress bar.

## Decisions the user may want to contest

- **The selection pill slides** to the cursor's row (spring, about 4 frames to 90 %) rather than jumping, and is clamped to the viewport while the list catches up. Setting `motion.pill_spring` to 0 makes it jump.
- **Pill geometry:** 12 px tall in its row, radius 3, reaching 1 px past the list's text area on each side (`pill_out_l` and `pill_out_r`).
- **The v0.3 screens' selection** (until B10) is now a rounded pill of the row's full width, redrawn on each move, instead of the flat tinted row.
- **Lists in cards:** rows inset 8 px from the screen edge, text 4 px into the row (`pad` 4); the scroll bar thumb at x 229.
- **Look values read off the mockups** where spec.md is silent:
  - heading line top 1, right-hand text top 2 and 64 px wide;
  - storage bar 5 px with round ends;
  - 3 px between a path pill and its "/";
  - 3 px between a section label's icon and its text;
  - a reading view's key column 44 px.
- **Path pills:** parents in a paper-coloured pill with faint text, the current folder in the tint with neutral ink. The mockup shows the item's ink on the tint, which spec.md's rule ("row text on pills is always neutral ink") and the 3.2–4.0:1 contrast speak against.
- **Hint bar:** chips right-aligned with 8 px between hints. The explanation line (T25) replaces the chips; it doesn't share the bar with them.
- **Palette:** light ink3 is darker (`#8C847B`), and the dark NES ink (the danger colour) lighter (`#EF7B84`). Every faint text in light, and NES marks and danger text in dark, look slightly different. `palette.png` in docs/design wasn't redrawn.
- **Sprites hide:**
  - while a dialog that has sprites fades in or out over them, or while a dialog with its own pill fades;
  - past the middle of a screen transition's alpha fade.
  They pop rather than fade at those moments, because the hardware can't blend a sprite that sits on a first-target plane.
- **Text over the pill during a page fade** blends with the full-colour pill (the GBA blends two layers only), so letters show faintly in the pill's colour for the 4 fade frames.

## Bugs

- **Screen entrances run close to a frame:** `ui-transition` and `lists-menu-open` reach 268,717 of 280,896 cycles. These are budget-capped hidden first draws (`UI_DRAW_BUDGET` 250k), and nodes drawn after the list plus the commit add to them. An overrun drops a frame only behind a transition. Lowering `UI_DRAW_BUDGET` to about 230k would add margin, but entrances would land a frame later more often. I left it for B19/B20.
- **`tr-tab`'s `settings2-in.png` and `settings2-back.png` now catch a plain backdrop frame:** the v0.3 settings page 2 comes in one frame later (its first draw is heavier). The golden is still deterministic. The screen goes in B10.
- **A pill or title icon whose y passes 160 during some future vertical slide** would wrap to the top. The title icon is culled, and the pill is clamped into its viewport, so neither shows today.
- **Pre-existing, `label_draw` (`ui_widgets.c`):** a one-set label redraws in place, so it may tear for a frame, and after a hide it is remapped only because the core sets `UI_N_DRAWN` after every draw. The new paints track their own `mapped` flag; labels were left as they are.
