**The user's follow-up** (after the first version): "take official artwork, and remove the actual art, keeping only the non art part of the box, and insert torii there". Asked where the official boxes should come from and whether they may be committed, the user chose to build them on the PC, never committed. So the placeholders now come in two layers:

1. **The card's own placeholder per console**, `/torii/art/box-<console>.bin`.
   - `just art-scrape` makes it from one real libretro-thumbnails box per console, cached in `dist/art-cache` like any picture: GBA *Metroid Fusion (USA)*, GBC *Wario Land 3*, GB *Kirby's Dream Land*, NES *Excitebike*.
   - At 80x80 the box's artwork and title are replaced by the skeleton (flat fill, pale shimmer, ground, torii gate, two title bars). The band, logos and the GBC ONLY FOR corner stay.
   - NES is cropped to its top square, as the list crops real NES covers: the black frame, the skeleton picture, and bars on black.
   - `tools/art_boxes.py` holds only the picture names and rectangles. Nothing of Nintendo's is in the repo or the kernel.
2. **The kernel's own drawn boxes** (below). They are the fallback when the card has no placeholder: no `art-scrape` run, Box art off, or before the card's file has loaded.

**What changed:**

- **Placeholder boxes, one a console.** `assets/ui/boxes/` holds five 80x80 boxes in place of B2's single recoloured `box.png`:
  - GBA: a dark band down the left edge with GAME BOY ADVANCE reading up it.
  - GB: a grey top band with an italic GAME BOY, the DMG bezel's navy and magenta lines, and a red seal badge.
  - GBC: a white top band with GAME BOY over a five-colour COLOR, a teal corner badge and a teal rule.
  - NES: the black box, with skeleton title bars, a framed picture, the grid and the red seal.
  - other (any other console, or no game): a plain slate band.

  The art area of each is a skeleton: a muted torii gate on a flat fill under a pale diagonal shimmer, with grey title bars where the game's name would be, so it reads as "no art yet" rather than as art. The boxes keep their consoles' trade dress in both themes. In the dark theme, `art.toml`'s `box_dark` swaps the skeleton tones, the edge and the shadow for graphite ones, and dims the GB and GBC bands slightly.
- **Format.** Each box is an RGBA PNG of at most 15 colours. `just assets` writes `art_box_tiles[ART_BOX_COUNT]` and `art_box_palettes[ART_BOX_COUNT][2]` (light, dark), with ids `ART_BOX_GBA`... `ART_BOX_OTHER`. `art_box_colours(box)` picks the theme's palette. The old `ART_BOX_BAND/BODY/SHAPE` slots are gone.
- **Choosing the box.** `boxart_select(row, box)` takes an `ART_BOX_*` id instead of a palette. `boxart.c` re-uploads the tiles when the box changes, for example between a GB and a GBA row in Recent or Favourites. `games.c` maps each console to its box (`console_box[]`) and uses `ART_BOX_OTHER` when the list has no game. Its RAM palette copy (`box_palette`, 32 bytes of EWRAM) is gone.
- **Icons.** The 16x16 icons redrawn:
  - Settings: a symmetric eight-tooth gear with a solid dark hub inside a light ring, replacing the uneven teeth and the slot-shaped hole.
  - Info: the same coin with a rim lit evenly from the top left.
  - NOR: a clean chip body with a pin-1 recess, replacing the stray dash marks.
  - SD card: the cut corner as a clean diagonal, three contacts, and a label that says SD.
  - Torii: the kasagi and footings move from near-black #212118 to a plum #7A5560, about 2.8:1 on graphite, so the beam shows on the dark theme.

  The 8x8 GBA (a handheld outline with a flat top for the shoulder buttons and a screen, no longer an O) and Info (a taller i) are redrawn too. Every other icon is unchanged. KyleBing's outlines are still used for the GBA, GBC and GB carousel objects, credited as before.
- **The card's placeholders in the kernel.**
  - `artkey` gains `ARTKEY_BOX` (`box-<name>.bin`, keyed by the console name).
  - `boxart.c` looks it up for the cursor's console when the game has no art of its own (`cursor_cover`).
  - It also always wants that placeholder after the neighbours, so the next game without art shows it at once (`BOXART_WANTS` 3 → 4).
  - `art_box_names` comes from `just assets`.
- **The card's placeholders on the PC.** `art_scrape.py`'s `scrape_boxes` makes placeholders only for the consoles the card has games of, and prints a `placeholders:` line.
  - A missing one is made, fetching its box if need be.
  - One on the card is remade from the cache and rewritten only when it differs. Without a cached box it is kept.
  - `art.palette_of` gained a colour-count parameter.
- **Tools.** `tools/mockup/boxes.py` draws the boxes, so a tweak is an edit to that script plus a run. `tools/mockup/icons_sheet.py REF OUT` draws before-and-after sheets against any revision. New design files: `docs/design/v0.4/icons-c2.png` (before and after, light and dark, at 4x) and `boxes-c2.png`.

**Deviations:**

- The boxes' trade dress keeps its own colours in both themes, and so do the icons (B2's rule). Only the skeleton, the box edge and the shadow follow the theme, through `box_dark`.
- The boxes are drawn by a script, not by hand in a paint program. The PNGs remain the converter's input.

**Decisions the user may want to contest:**

- **Template boxes.** Metroid Fusion (GBA), Wario Land 3 (GBC), Kirby's Dream Land (GB) and Excitebike (NES), all US boxes. They are swapped in `tools/art_boxes.py` by a name plus the rectangle that is replaced.
- **What is kept.** Only the band and the corner badge. The Nintendo seals, logo and ratings in the art area go: at 80 px they drag pieces of the game's art along with them.
- **Skeleton colours.** The card's placeholders are covers, so they use one palette in both themes: a light grey-lavender skeleton on the GB, GBC and GBA boxes, and a dark one in the NES black box.
- **Rebuilt every run.** `art-scrape` rebuilds placeholders every run from the cache, so a hand-made `box-gba.bin` is replaced if the cached box differs. Delete the cached box to keep your own.
- **The kernel's drawn boxes stay as the fallback.** You turned their look down. They only show without the card's placeholder, and for consoles with no template (`other`). They also show for about 8 frames the first time, before the card's placeholder has loaded.

- **Box layouts.** GBA uses the vertical left band; GB and GBC use a top band with a badge; NES is the black box; everything else gets a slate band. The logos are my own pixel lettering, not Nintendo's logos. GBC's COLOR uses five letter colours. GB's badge is a red seal, not the Nintendo wordmark.
- **The skeleton look.** A muted torii gate on a flat fill, a pale diagonal shimmer, and two title bars. Nothing animates: the shimmer is still, and a moving one would cost VRAM uploads every frame.
- **Dark theme.** The skeleton turns graphite (#303038 fill, #484850 gate). The box edge turns near-black, and the GB and GBC bands dim (#D0D0C8, #A0A890). The NES box stays black on the dark card, where its picture window is the only lighter part.
- **Size.** Each box is 79x79 with a 1 px drop shadow to the right and below, inside the 80x80 slot, so it sits 1 px smaller than a real cover.
- **An empty list** (no game) shows the plain `other` box.
- **Torii glyph.** The beam and footings are now plum #7A5560, not near-black, which loses some of the black-kasagi look in light. This also changes the carousel's top-left glyph.
- **SD card icon.** The label now says "SD".
- **8x8 GBA icon.** It is now an outline handheld: a flat top for the shoulders, a solid screen and a rounded base, like the GB and GBC marks.

**Review:** one code reviewer. Fixed in `c444c4b`:

- With Box art off, a change of placeholder that the full VRAM queue put off was never retried. `boxart_poll` returned before `sync`.
- `boxart_open` uploaded the previous page's box before the first row was selected.
- A console added to `LIB_*` would silently get the GB box. A `_Static_assert` now fails the build until it has one.
- The kernel test took pure black for an undefined colour. It now checks that a box's colour indices are contiguous.
- No golden showed a placeholder in the dark theme. `games-art-dark/gba-none` now does.

Left as they are:

- The palette can land one frame before the tiles on a box-to-box switch while the queue is busy. Covers already have this.
- No test covers placeholder-to-placeholder with no cover in between, for example GB then GBA in Recent. The scenarios' Recent lists hold only GBA games.

Second review, of the card placeholders. Fixed in `76907ae`:

- Placeholders were made for every console, not only those with games.
- Placeholders were rewritten on every run.
- "missing" was printed where the box was only not fetched.
- The art-limit warning didn't count the box files.
- The placeholder was wanted at the neighbours' priority.
- `artkey_parse` checked CRC names before box names, so a four-letter console name would never have parsed.

Left as they are:

- When a game's own art file is broken, it shows the drawn box, not the card's placeholder.
- In Recent and Favourites, a move to another console's game doesn't prefetch that console's placeholder. It loads when the cursor rests.

**Bugs:** none found beyond the reviews'.
