# D2 One placeholder box per console, redrawn

**Branch and commits:** `claude/v04-d2-boxes-92fdc8`, on `origin/v0.4` (`0331838`; nothing newer to rebase onto).

- `78ba864` D2: drop the card's placeholder boxes (box-<console>.bin)
- `c83275d` D2: redraw the placeholder boxes
- `ac5fe69` D2: art-scrape also deletes the old placeholders' macOS ._ files
- `0c2ffb0` D2: placeholder boxes after the real US boxes
- the commit adding this report

**What changed:**

- **One fallback.** The user tested C2 on a cart. The "ugly border black bg GBA template" was C2's card placeholder, `/torii/art/box-<console>.bin`, which `just art-scrape` built from an official box. It is gone; the kernel's drawn boxes are the only placeholder.
  - Kernel: `ARTKEY_BOX` is gone from `artkey.{c,h}`, and `box_cover`/`cursor_cover` from `boxart.c`. `BOXART_WANTS` is back to `1 + 2 * BOXART_AHEAD` (3), and `art_box_names` is no longer generated. A `box-*.bin` left on a card parses as `ARTKEY_NONE`, so it is not listed and never loaded.
  - Card tool: `art_scrape.py` deletes C2's `box-gba/gbc/gb/nes.bin` instead of making them. It also deletes the `._` files macOS wrote beside them, matches names in any case, ignores `--console`, and only lists them on `--dry-run`. `tools/art_boxes.py` is deleted, and `art.palette_of`'s colour-count parameter is reverted.
- **The boxes, redrawn** (`tools/mockup/boxes.py` → `assets/ui/boxes/*.png`). After the user's mid-session note ("GBA sidebar is not dark … as original as possible"), I compared the boxes against real US boxes in the art cache, used only to look at. Each box is now that layout redrawn in pixels at 80x80:
  - **GB:** a silver band down the left, with a big indigo GAME BOY reading up it under the red Nintendo mark. The Nintendo oval sits at the bottom right.
  - **GBC:** the same band, with GAME BOY in indigo and COLOR in its five colours, and the black ONLY FOR corner at the bottom right (with five COLOR dots).
  - **GBA:** the same light band, with GAME BOY (thin) ADVANCE (bold) in grey. The publisher mark sits at the bottom right.
  - **NES:** the black box. Its sprite on the grid is a pixel torii before a muted red sun, with the Nintendo seal and the system logo bar at the bottom.
  - **Other:** a plain slate band.
  - **Skeleton** (GB, GBC, GBA, other): title bars at the top of the art, a muted vermilion torii gate before a pale sun over low hills, and the rating box on the ground at the bottom left. It is the same in each box. C2's diagonal shimmer is gone.
  - **Dark theme** (`art.toml` `box_dark`): the skeleton turns graphite with a dusky rose gate. The silver band dims slightly (#C8C8C8) but stays light. The NES box is identical in both themes.
- **Design sheet:** `docs/design/v0.4/boxes-d2.png` replaces `boxes-c2.png`. For each theme it shows C2's boxes above D2's, at 1x, then the same at 4x. It is drawn by `boxes.py SHEET REF`, which reads the "before" boxes and dark map from git revision REF.

**Decisions the user may want to contest:**

- **All three Game Boy boxes use the silver left band.** That is how US GB, GBC and GBA boxes look. It replaces C2's top bands, and with them the DMG navy and magenta lines and the teal GBC corner. The band is light in both themes (dimmed in dark).
- **Lettering:** the GB's GAME BOY is a 7-pixel bold, and the GBC and GBA use 5-pixel letters (ADVANCE and COLOR wider). These are my own pixel letters, not Nintendo's logos.
- **The skeleton:** a muted vermilion torii, a pale sun and low hills, with no shimmer. Its title bars and rating box are the gate's colour.
- **NES:** a pixel torii and a red sun on the black grid, with no picture window. Its sun is the only saturated colour in any skeleton.
- **Other consoles** keep a slate top band with the title in it.
- **art-scrape** deletes C2's placeholders on every run, for all consoles whatever `--console` says. It never makes them again.
