# v0.4 decisions to review

Every choice the v0.4 build made that you didn't explicitly agree to and that changes what you see, feel or store. Each is cheap to change: say the number and what you want. Detail and reasons are in [v0.4-decisions.md](v0.4-decisions.md) (research overrides R1–R9 and the ticket log); bugs and open questions are in the roadmap's v0.4.2 section.

## Behaviour

1. **A starts games with your addons.** A, Play and Launch use the addons Settings turns on; Clean boot is its own menu entry. v0.3's A, A was a clean boot. GB, GBC and NES always start clean.
2. **Hard reset:** hold L as you pick Play (or Launch) in a menu, or hold L and press A on a list (L pages first, then A starts the game shown).
3. **Keys during the opening zoom:** A, START and SELECT pressed before the page shows are dropped, so A, A from home stops on the page (T19's "A, A resumes Recent" needs a short pause).
4. **Keys while a popup closes:** moves reach the page; A, B, START and SELECT are dropped.
5. **A page change fades out, then in:** about 9 frames instead of 6.
6. **Settings are written once on leaving,** after a short black pause; Time and Date set the clock at once; edits are lost if you power off on the page.
7. **The boot position is saved after 5 s idle,** a small SD write in the menu.
8. **No folder-date check:** boot shows the menu at once and a background walk checks the card (R1).
9. **User files are written as two copies** (`NAME` and `NAME.bak`, each with a CRC line), never renamed (R2). Hand-edited files without a CRC line are accepted.
10. **Rename's keyboard:** B deletes and never cancels (use the Cancel key, or START on an unchanged name). Rename is offered for SD games the index holds, not NOR or hidden games. Renaming an SD file moves its save, save type, save states, cheats and custom art with it.
11. **Delete keeps the save** everywhere; NOR deletes only the latest game.
12. **No hold-A on Format all:** a Cancel-preselected question, as for every destructive action.
13. **Favourites are capped at 4,096** (you said no cap).
14. **Covers wait for a resting cursor** (8 frames, then one row each side) and pause while a popup is up.
15. **Names database:** `just names-update` computes every game's CRC on the PC (a launch can't: the cart copies the ROM without the CPU). A GBA romhack missing from No-Intro shows its base game's name and art by game code; custom names and art override.
16. **The art scraper** never refreshes existing art and stops at the first timeout (a rerun resumes).

## Lists and names

17. **Sort order:** case and accents folded, numbers by value ("Mega Man 2" before "Mega Man 10"); a leading "The" counts (R3).
18. **Name cleaning** (R4, B4): underscores become spaces only in names without spaces; 4–5 digit scene prefixes go, except 1900–2099 (years); the article table includes French, German, Spanish, Italian and Dutch articles and can misfire ("Rock, Paper, A"); colliding games show their first differing tag, preferring Rev, Beta, version and translation tags.
19. **Hidden on the SD page:** `torii`, `SAVER`, `RTS`, `SYSTEM`, `PATCH`, `CHEAT`, `System Volume Information`, dot files, FAT hidden/system files, and every file the kernel can't start (`.sav`, `.txt`, `.cht`). `.sgb` and dot-less files no longer launch.
20. **SD folders:** files sort by name without the extension; a folder over 1,024 files shows the first 1,024 the card lists (folders past it stay reachable); counts are recounted on each visit.
21. **The game popup:** Play, Favourite, Clean boot, Boot with addon (the cheat screen when there's a cheat file; greyed out with every addon off), Rename, Game info, Delete. Game info is a page with Save type; list with art shows two facts (Size, Last played).
22. **The "played ago" words:** "just now", "5m ago", "2h ago", "yesterday", "3 days", "1 week", "1 month", "1 year"; nothing while the clock is unset (before 2026-10-01).
23. **Start on** offers Last place, Recent, Home, Favourites, SD card and the consoles (not NOR, Settings, Info); a hidden choice falls back to home facing the nearest item.
24. **Hotkeys:** 2–3 keys including L, R, START or SELECT; no overlap with the other hotkey even while it's off; nothing inside A+B+START+SELECT. With Save states on, the reset keys are armed whatever Reset hotkey says (as in v0.3).

## Look

25. **Palette changed for contrast:** light ink3 `#8C847B` (was `#9C948C`), dark danger red `#EF7B84` (was `#E76B73`).
26. **16x16 icons keep their own colours** instead of taking the item's accent, the same in light and dark.
27. **Carousel:** neighbours at 0.89 scale and the far pair at 0.60 (spec's formula); far dark objects fog almost to nothing in dark; with 2–5 items one item briefly shows at both ends mid-turn; objects keep their own sizes (GBA 64x40 at the front).
28. **Theme wipe:** a hard edge, 16 frames, reversible (R7: a dithered edge would need more IWRAM).
29. **Opening:** the object zooms in place to 1.9x and fades into the paper; the title slides in, the card rises 12 px; closing is 5 + 5 frames.
30. **Dialogs:** questions and errors rise in over 6 frames; the loading card shows at once; the confirmation dims the page toward white in light (R9).
31. **Loading card:** titled with the file name; one bar for the whole start; status only for Loading, Patching and Writing to NOR; the copy-speed estimate (64 KB a frame) is a guess until measured on your cart.
32. **Look values read off the mockups** where the spec is silent: shadows 1 px, scroll bar 3 px, hint chips (right-aligned, explanation lines centred), pill 12 px with radius 3 on a fast spring (it jumps on Settings).
33. **Popups:** the chosen item in ink on the tint, others ink2; values in the page's ink (not pink); the letter card 38x38 at the list's top right; L/R page 8 rows.
34. **Pages that deviate from their mockup:** NOR (one card, four rows, no dots), SD (path pills in the title bar, folder icons blue), Settings (sections 5 px further apart), Info (14 px reading rows).
35. **The Settings and keyboard mockups** were approved by me for the build: `docs/design/v0.4/settings-*.png`, `keyboard-*.png`.
